# Meta Ad Review Recovery Notes

Use this note when retrying PawPeaceGuide ads rejected under `Discriminatory Practices` or `Financial products and services`.

## What Meta Flagged

Meta classified the ads as promoting financial and insurance products or services. For PawPeaceGuide, that is expected because the site educates users about pet insurance and routes users to third-party provider quote pages through affiliate links.

Do not appeal unchanged. Edit the campaign or ad set first.

## Required Retry Setup

- Set Special Ad Category to `Financial products and services`.
- Target only Accounts Center accounts age 18+.
- Use broad, non-sensitive targeting.
- Avoid ZIP-level targeting, protected-class proxies, personal hardship targeting, and exclusion stacks.
- Send traffic to `https://pawpeaceguide.com/pet-parent-protection`.
- Use education-first copy and happy pet imagery.
- Do not imply the viewer has financial problems.
- Do not imply the viewer's pet is currently sick.
- Do not use emergency, crisis, graphic, or pressure-heavy creative for the retry.
- Do not directly request PII, bank details, card details, routing numbers, Social Security numbers, full applications, or sensitive health records on the paid landing page.
- Do not claim discounted insurance, large savings, guaranteed approval, guaranteed coverage, or request any upfront fee on PawPeaceGuide.

## Why Odie Still Works

Odie remains the current live monetized path, but PawPeaceGuide should not force a provider click directly from the ad. The paid landing page sends visitors into education and path selection first. Dog and cat visitors who are ready to review quote options can then continue through `/go/odie`, with affiliate disclosure before clickout.
