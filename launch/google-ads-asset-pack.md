# Google Ads Asset Pack

Use this pack to improve Performance Max and search ad strength without making claims PawPeaceGuide cannot support.

## Landing Page

Primary paid-search URL:

```text
https://pawpeaceguide.com/pet-insurance?utm_source=google&utm_medium=paid_search&utm_campaign=pet_insurance_search_test&utm_content=general
```

Use pet-specific URLs only when the ad group or asset group is clearly pet-specific:

```text
https://pawpeaceguide.com/dog-insurance-options?utm_source=google&utm_medium=paid_search&utm_campaign=dog_insurance_search_test&utm_content=dog
https://pawpeaceguide.com/cat-insurance-options?utm_source=google&utm_medium=paid_search&utm_campaign=cat_insurance_search_test&utm_content=cat
https://pawpeaceguide.com/senior-pet-insurance?utm_source=google&utm_medium=paid_search&utm_campaign=senior_pet_search_test&utm_content=senior
```

Do not send ads directly to `/go/odie` or any other `/go/[provider]` route.

## Conversion Reality Check

A few dollars of spend without a provider clickout is not enough data to decide the funnel is failing. Watch:

- Search terms that triggered spend
- Landing-page bounce or engaged-session rate
- `/quiz` starts
- `/calculator` starts
- `/ready-to-compare` views
- `/go/odie` clicks
- Cost per `/go/odie` click

Pause or exclude terms that produce research-only visitors with no quiz, calculator, compare, or Odie click behavior.
